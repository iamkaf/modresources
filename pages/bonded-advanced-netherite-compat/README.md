**This add-on is retired, because Bonded does its job now.** From Bonded 5.0.0 on Minecraft 1.21.11 and newer, [Advanced Netherite](https://modrinth.com/mod/advanced-netherite) support is built into Bonded's Gear Rules. Install [Bonded](https://modrinth.com/mod/bonded) and Advanced Netherite, and you're set. Thanks to everyone who used this add-on!

## Older versions

The older builds make [Advanced Netherite](https://modrinth.com/mod/advanced-netherite) gear work with [Bonded](https://modrinth.com/mod/bonded). Its tools and weapons gain Bond like their vanilla counterparts, and its items show Bonded's level and progress in their tooltips.

Requires [Bonded](https://modrinth.com/mod/bonded) and [Advanced Netherite](https://modrinth.com/mod/advanced-netherite). Fabric files also require [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).

Available for Fabric and NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21.1 and 1.21.4.

![An advanced netherite item with the Bonded tooltip](https://i.kaf.sh/i/6c269168-3d23-4816-8021-0779d5a1bf73.png)

To make another gear mod work with Bonded, use Gear Rules or let me know.
