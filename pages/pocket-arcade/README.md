Pocket Arcade adds a handheld game console you craft, carry, and play anywhere. It starts out with a 1-bit screen and two games. Each upgrade adds two more games and redraws the ones you already have in more color, up to a full-color console with eight games.

Available for Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2, and 26.3. Requires [Amber](https://modrinth.com/mod/amber) and [Konfig](https://modrinth.com/mod/konfig). Fabric files also require [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api). Install it on both the client and the server.

## Four generations

Use the console to hold it up and play. The world keeps going while you do, so find a safe spot first.

Upgrade it by crafting it with a chip. The same game looks like this on each generation:

![Creeper Dash at the same moment on all four consoles: green-grey 1-bit, four shades of green, sixteen colors, and full color with a sunset and mountains](UPLOAD:generations.webp)

- **Pocket Arcade:** a 1-bit LCD with Cart Chain and Slime Pong. Craft it from copper, glass, redstone, and a button.
- **Pocket Arcade Plus:** four shades, and adds Creeper Dash and Brick Miner. Upgrade with a Shade Chip: gold, redstone, and nether quartz.
- **Pocket Arcade Color:** sixteen colors, and adds Ore Match and Phantom Raid. Upgrade with a Color Chip: diamonds, amethyst, lapis, and redstone.
- **Pocket Arcade Prism:** full color with parallax, particles, and glow, and adds Elytra Run and End Climb. Upgrade with a Prism Chip: an echo shard, amethyst, a diamond, and glow ink sacs.

Your high scores carry over when you upgrade.

## Eight games

![All eight games running on the Prism console](UPLOAD:games-store.webp)

- **Cart Chain:** steer a minecart through a cave and collect ore to couple on more carts. Don't hit the walls or your own train.
- **Slime Pong:** bounce a slime past the other paddle. Every set you win makes the next one faster.
- **Creeper Dash:** jump cacti and duck under phantoms for as long as you can.
- **Brick Miner:** break through layers of blocks with a bouncing ball, and catch the power-ups ores drop.
- **Ore Match:** swap gems to line up three or more before the clock runs out. Clear four for TNT.
- **Phantom Raid:** shoot down waves of phantoms from the castle wall.
- **Elytra Run:** glide through a winding cave and fire rockets for a burst of speed.
- **End Climb:** bounce up through the End and throw ender pearls to warp higher.

Your movement keys or the arrow keys are the D-pad. Jump or Enter is A, and sneak or Backspace is B. Escape powers the console off.

## High scores

Each console keeps its best score for every game, along with who set it. Hover the console to see them. When you beat one, everyone nearby sees it pop up over your head.

## Arcade TV

Use a console on an Arcade TV to dock it. Use the TV with an empty hand to play the docked console, and everyone nearby can watch the game on the screen. One player can play at a time. Sneak and use the TV to take your console back.

## Arcade Manual

With [Modonomicon](https://modrinth.com/mod/modonomicon) installed, craft a book with a stone button to get the Arcade Manual. It covers the consoles, upgrades, and the TV, and how to play every game.

## Configuration

Open the config screen from Mod Menu on Fabric or the mod list on Forge and NeoForge. You can turn off the fading LCD trail on the Mono and Plus screens and set how loud console sounds are.
