Veteran Golems lets your golems get better at the jobs they already do. Iron golems that keep fighting earn ranks, copper golems sort faster once you give them amethyst, and snow golems packed with ice throw snowballs that actually hurt. Every upgrade shows on the golem, so you can spot a veteran from across the village.

## Iron golems: Veteran

An iron golem that kills 25 hostile mobs becomes a Veteran. It gets gold rank chevrons and battle scars, and its punches deal 5 more damage (20 instead of 15). Mobs it flings to their death count too.

Sneak and use an empty hand on an iron golem to see how many kills it has and which ranks it holds.

![An iron golem ranks up after finishing a zombie: an iron sword and the word Veteran rise over it](https://i.kaf.sh/i/5e18e52c-9476-4180-ae5e-c16c855b43d0.webp)

## Iron golems: Raid Hardened

An iron golem that hits a raider during a raid and is still standing when the raid is won comes out Raid Hardened. It wears a captured raid banner as a tabard and cape, and its max health goes from 100 to 150. A golem that only watched the raid gets nothing.

A golem can be both a Veteran and Raid Hardened.

![Two Raid Hardened iron golems, one showing its banner tabard and the other its cape](https://i.kaf.sh/i/ba2039a5-4cb1-44c3-bd94-962a2b93f997.webp)

## Copper golems: Attuned

Use an amethyst shard on a copper golem to attune it. Its antenna grows into an amethyst crystal, it walks 50% faster, and it spends about half as long at each chest. The shard is used up. A copper golem that oxidizes all the way into a statue loses the attunement, so wax the ones you care about.

![An attuned copper golem with an amethyst antenna next to a plain copper golem and a copper chest](https://i.kaf.sh/i/936bd530-17e3-4e93-aea3-cbf34dbc6019.webp)

## Snow golems: Frosted

Use ice, packed ice, or blue ice on a snow golem to frost it. Each tier covers it in more ice:

| Ice | Snowballs per throw | Damage to hostile mobs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ice | 1 | 1 |
| Packed ice | 2 | 2 |
| Blue ice | 3 | 3, and Slowness for 2 seconds |

You can skip straight to blue ice, and a golem never goes back down a tier. Frosted snowballs only hurt hostile mobs: players, villagers, pets, and other golems still take no damage.

![Four snow golems in a row, from plain to blue ice armour](https://i.kaf.sh/i/a8b465cb-a614-4d01-ba6b-62864062ef1d.webp)

## Configuration

Open the config screen from Mod Menu on Fabric or the mod list on Forge and NeoForge. Hover any setting to see a picture of what it changes. You can turn each upgrade off and change how many kills make a Veteran. Golems keep upgrades they already earned.

![The Veteran Golems config screen, sliding through a picture for each setting](https://i.kaf.sh/i/0c9561f4-c448-4725-a5eb-123a79024840.webp)

## Current support

Veteran Golems runs on Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge for Minecraft 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2, and 26.3. Each Minecraft version also gets one jar that runs on all three loaders. Install it on both the client and the server. It requires [Amber](https://modrinth.com/mod/amber) and [Konfig](https://modrinth.com/mod/konfig), and on Fabric also [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).
