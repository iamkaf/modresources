**This add-on is retired, because Bonded does its job now.** From Bonded 5.0.0 on Minecraft 1.21.11 and newer, [Basic Weapons](https://modrinth.com/mod/basic-weapons) support is built into Bonded's Gear Rules. Install [Bonded](https://modrinth.com/mod/bonded) and Basic Weapons, and you're set. Thanks to everyone who used this add-on!

## Older versions

The older builds make [Basic Weapons](https://modrinth.com/mod/basic-weapons) work with [Bonded](https://modrinth.com/mod/bonded). Its daggers, glaives, quarterstaffs, clubs, spears, and hammers gain Bond in combat like other melee weapons, and the Tool Bench can upgrade them to the next tier.

Requires [Bonded](https://modrinth.com/mod/bonded) and [Basic Weapons](https://modrinth.com/mod/basic-weapons). Fabric files also require [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).

Available for Fabric on Minecraft 1.21, 1.21.1, and 1.21.4, and NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21 and 1.21.1.

![A basic weapon with the Bonded tooltip](https://i.kaf.sh/i/a7fee907-8fdb-4fbb-931b-d0c9da92cd4b.png)

To make another gear mod work with Bonded, use Gear Rules or let me know.
