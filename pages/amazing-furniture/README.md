Amazing Furniture adds a Carpenter's Bench and fourteen pieces of furniture you make at it: chairs, stools, benches, armchairs, sofas, tables, coffee tables, desks, nightstands, cabinets, wardrobes, lamps, counters, and kitchen cabinets. Each one comes in every vanilla wood, and cushions and lampshades come in every wool color.

Available for Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge on Minecraft 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2, and 26.3. Requires [Amber](https://modrinth.com/mod/amber). Fabric files also require [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api). Install it on both the server and the client.

## The Carpenter's Bench

Craft the bench from three planks, two sticks, and an iron ingot. Put planks in its top slot to pick the wood and wool in the second slot to pick the fabric. Then click a piece in the catalog. The piece you would make turns slowly in the window on the right, in the materials you put in, and appears in the result slot once the slots hold enough. Hover a piece to see what it costs. Shift-click the result to make as many as your materials allow.

Counters and kitchen cabinets take a stone block in the second slot instead of wool, and it becomes their countertop: smooth stone, polished andesite, diorite, granite, deepslate, blackstone, or tuff, or a block of quartz.

![The Carpenter's Bench with cherry planks and red wool in its slots, the catalog in cherry and red, and a chair turning in the preview](https://i.kaf.sh/i/61f2f556-64e2-41e7-8922-85ec596e9a55.png)

## Every wood, one item

A cherry chair with red cushions and a birch chair with blue ones are the same item, so your creative tab and recipe viewer show fourteen pieces of furniture rather than hundreds. Breaking a piece gives back exactly what you placed. Furniture uses the textures of the planks, logs, wool, and stone it is made from, so a resource pack that changes those changes the furniture too.

Use a dye on a cushioned piece or a lamp to recolor it where it stands.

## What each piece does

- **Chairs, stools, benches, armchairs, and sofas** are seats. Use one with an empty hand to sit down, and sneak to stand up. Sofas and benches set side by side join into one long piece.
- **Tables and coffee tables** join with their own kind on any side, keeping legs only at the outer corners.
- **Desks and nightstands** hold one row of nine items, **cabinets** two rows, and **wardrobes** three. Wardrobes are two blocks tall. Hoppers can fill and empty all of them.
- **Counters** hold one row of nine and **kitchen cabinets** two, under their stone tops.
- **Lamps** give off as much light as a torch. Use one with an empty hand to switch it on or off.

Wooden furniture burns like planks do.

## Guidebook

With [Modonomicon](https://modrinth.com/mod/modonomicon) installed, every player gets a Furniture Catalog book the first time they join, explaining the bench and each piece. Amazing Furniture works the same without it.
