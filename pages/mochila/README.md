A colorful upgradable backpack mod for Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge.

Requires [Amber](https://modrinth.com/mod/amber) and [Konfig](https://modrinth.com/mod/konfig). Fabric files also
require [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api). Install it on both the client and the server.

![A gif of all the backpacks.](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/f4723d0598b20a3229b2d53f5c329c16f14947d5.gif)

### How To Use It

Craft the backpack and open it by right clicking it or by using the hotkey (default B).

Each tier holds more: leather has 2 rows, iron 3, gold 4, diamond 5, and netherite 6. Backpacks can be renamed in an anvil, dyed with any vanilla dye, and upgraded through the material tiers. Upgrades preserve the backpack's contents, name, mode, and color.

The Ender Backpack opens your ender chest. Right-click it or use its hotkey (default V).

### Quick Stashing

You can unload the contents of a backpack into a chest or barrel by sneaking and right-clicking it. It also works if there is an item frame or sign in front of it!

Backpacks have two modes. **Dump** moves everything. **Store** only moves items the container already has. Hold the backpack and press Shift+B to swap modes.

Mochila shows how many items were moved and which container received them. Chests, barrels, trapped chests, and copper chest variants work by default.

### Configuration

Open Mochila's settings from your loader's mod list. On Fabric, install [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) to add the configuration button.

You can turn quick stashing off, choose which blocks count as stash targets, and toggle its messages, sound, and particles. You can also hide the mode and size lines from backpack tooltips. On servers, the server's quick stash settings apply.
