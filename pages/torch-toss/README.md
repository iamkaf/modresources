Throw torches to light up caves and tunnels. Works with soul, copper, and redstone torches too.

Available for Fabric, Forge, and NeoForge. Requires [Amber](https://modrinth.com/mod/amber) and
[Konfig](https://modrinth.com/mod/konfig). Fabric files also require [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).

### How To Use It

Combine two torches on the crafting grid to make two throwable torches. Place one in your hand and right-click to throw it.

![Throwable torch recipe](https://i.kaf.sh/i/e7690d9f-651b-40c1-b79b-7694c55ef97b.png)

![Throwable soul torch recipe](https://i.kaf.sh/i/d1f541f6-1211-45a1-a782-586b764f1805.png)

![Throwable redstone torch recipe](https://i.kaf.sh/i/048996a9-16e6-4586-89de-ca15d996162a.png)

The torch places itself where it lands, on a wall or on the floor. If there's no room for it, it drops as an item
instead. A throwable redstone torch powers redstone like a placed one, so you can switch on a circuit from a distance.

Hitting a mob with a throwable torch deals 1 damage. Each torch type has its own damage setting, from 0 to 4.

Two throwable torches craft back into two regular torches. Throwable copper torches need Minecraft 1.21.10 or newer.

You can also find throwable torches in chests. Regular and redstone ones show up in dungeons, mineshafts, strongholds,
mansions, temples, igloos, buried treasure, pillager outposts, and village temples and cartographer houses. Soul torches
show up in Nether fortresses, bastions, and ancient cities, and copper torches in trial chamber rewards.

## Compatibility

With [LambDynamicLights](https://modrinth.com/mod/lambdynamiclights) installed, throwable torches give off light like
regular torches.
